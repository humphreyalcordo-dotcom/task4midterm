Adulthood

He grabbed her hand and pulled her up. She was shaking because her fear of heights was getting the best of her, how silly it's just a ladder she thought to herself. He made sure she was safe on the wooden platform that was below their feet. As she was about to sit down just far enough away from the edge, in a spot where she felt a little safe, he looked at her and reached for her hand. She looked at him, into his eyes, those piercing eyes that made her heart skip a beat, the same eyes that tell a story all in their own. She knew just from his look that she could trust him, she grabbed his hand and followed him a few steps to the edge. Together they sat with their feet dangling, her heart was racing frombeing so close to the edge. She looked up and suddenly slowly her heart slowed down.

My Favorites

My Favorite Artist

Jay R is my favorite singer because of his amazing voice and the way he expresses emotions through his music. I enjoy listening to his songs because they are relaxing, meaningful, and easy to relate to. His smooth singing style makes his songs enjoyable, especially when I want to relax or think about different experiences in life. I also admire his talent and passion for music. For me, Jay R is more than just a singer because his music can bring emotions and memories that make his songs special.

My Favorite Food

Sinigang is my favorite food because it has a delicious sour and savory taste that I really enjoy. I like how the warm soup, vegetables, and meat come together to make a comforting and satisfying meal. It is especially enjoyable to eat with rice and is perfect for sharing with family. For me, sinigang is not only delicious but also reminds me of home and family meals.

My Favorite Place

Prague is my favorite place because of its beautiful architecture, historic buildings, and peaceful atmosphere. I love how the city combines old European charm with modern life. The streets, bridges, and beautiful views make Prague an amazing place to explore and take pictures. I would love to visit Prague someday because it looks like a magical and memorable place.
